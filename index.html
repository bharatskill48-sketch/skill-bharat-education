Trade-wise Learning Notes / ট্রেডভিত্তিক পড়াশোনা

These are starter study notes for revision. Follow your instructor's syllabus and safety procedures.

Electrical

Topics: electrical safety, voltage/current/resistance, Ohm's law, series and parallel circuits, AC/DC, fuse/MCB, earthing, multimeter basics.

Quick note: Ohm's law: V = I × R. Switch off and isolate the supply before working; verify absence of voltage with a suitable tester. Never work on live mains circuits as a beginner.

Practice: Identify phase, neutral and earth on a training board; calculate current for a simple low-voltage circuit.

AC Technician

Topics: refrigeration cycle, compressor, condenser, evaporator, expansion device, thermostat, airflow, filters, basic fault diagnosis.

Quick note: The basic cycle moves heat through evaporation and condensation of refrigerant. Poor cooling can come from dirty filters/coils, airflow problems, incorrect settings or refrigerant-system faults.

Practice: Clean a filter, inspect airflow and record inlet/outlet temperatures. Refrigerant and electrical work should be done only with appropriate training and equipment.

Computer / IT

Topics: computer components, operating systems, files/folders, networking, IP address, Wi-Fi, troubleshooting, cybersecurity and backups.

Quick note: Troubleshoot from simple to complex: check power and cables, restart safely, read the error message, test connectivity, then change one setting at a time.

Practice: Create folders and backups, identify CPU/RAM/storage, and use network settings to find the device's IP address.

Mobile Repairing

Topics: phone components, battery/charging, display, connectors, software reset, ESD safety, water damage and fault diagnosis.

Quick note: Confirm the symptom and inspect the charging cable/adapter before opening a device. Power off, disconnect the battery where appropriate and use ESD protection.

Practice: Record symptoms, inspect ports with magnification, test with known-good accessories and back up user data before software work. Do not puncture or heat a lithium battery.

General Trade

Topics: workshop safety, hand tools, measuring tools, marking, cutting, drilling, fastening, material handling and housekeeping.

Quick note: Select the right tool for the job, secure the workpiece, wear task-appropriate PPE and check tools for damage before use.

Practice: Read a ruler/tape measure, mark a straight line, identify common tools and prepare a short job-safety checklist.

Plumber / Plumbing

Topics: pipe and fitting types, measuring/cutting, joints, taps and valves, drainage, traps, leaks, water pressure and sanitation.

Quick note: Shut off the water supply before repairs. A trap holds water to block sewer gases; drainage needs the correct fall and secure joints to avoid leaks.

Practice: Identify elbows, tees, couplings and valves; measure and mark a pipe; inspect a training joint for leaks. Follow local plumbing codes and hygiene practices.

Note: These are introductory notes, not complete trade textbooks or a substitute for supervised practical training.
